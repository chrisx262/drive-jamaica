# Art Direction — Drive Jamaica

**Status: DRAFT for owner review.** This is assembled from the owner's route descriptions plus general knowledge of Jamaica's look. The owner's lived knowledge overrides anything here — correct freely. The single best way to anchor this doc: owner drops route photos into `assets/reference/` (phone pics, Google Street View screenshots, YouTube drive-through stills all fine — they guide the voxel work, they don't ship in the game).

## Core principle
Low-poly / voxel stylization (MagicaVoxel/Blockbench per GAME_SPEC), but every choice should make a Jamaican say "mi know dat place." Recognition beats realism; color and silhouette carry the feeling.

## Palette (working values)
- **Lush green** everywhere — Jamaica is green first: `#3a7d2c` canopy, `#4e8c3a` grass, `#2d5d23` deep bush
- **Zinc roofs** — silver-grey `#9aa0a6`, rust streaks `#8c5a3c`; a few painted red `#a63d2f`
- **Concrete + board houses** — cream `#e8e0c9`, sky blue `#7fb5d5`, mint `#9fd6b1`, pink `#e8a9b8`; weathered, not pastel-clean
- **Jamaican accents** — flag green `#007749`, gold `#fed100`, black; used on signs, road markings, kit
- **Sea** — harbour calm `#2e7fb8`, open rough `#1c5a86`, north-coast turquoise near Ochi `#2ec4b6`
- **Earth** — red dirt cuttings `#a0522d`, limestone gorge rock `#c9bfa8`, sand `#e3d29f`

## Look of each leg (V1 route)
1. **Palisadoes** — bare windswept spit: sea both sides, low scrub, the airport behind, mountains ahead. Pristine asphalt (owner: government maintains the airport road; potholes start after Harbour View roundabout). **Owner-supplied video reference:** [Gunboat Beach / Harbour View to NMIA, Palisadoes Road](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9ywoOqT-Mk) — real footage of this exact leg.
2. **Windward Rd → Mountain View Ave** — dense Kingston: zinc fences, walled yards, shop fronts with painted ads, route taxis, National Stadium.
3. **Trafalgar/Hope Rd → Half Way Tree** — uptown: bigger trees, Devon House lawns, plazas, the clock tower, traffic.
4. **Hagley Park Rd → Spanish Town Rd** — commercial/industrial grit, market sprawl, busy junctions.
5. **Mandela Hwy → Spanish Town** — flat plains, cane and scrub, old town square with Georgian edges.
6. **Bog Walk gorge** — the drama: sheer limestone walls, Rio Cobre alongside the road, overhanging bush, Flat Bridge one-lane moment.
7. **Linstead → Ewarton** — market-town main streets ("Linstead Market" energy), then the climb.
8. **Mount Rosser / Faith's Pen** — switchbacks in mist-green hills; Faith's Pen = row of smoking jerk stalls, zinc roofs, hand-painted signs.
9. **Moneague → Walkers Wood** — cool-country farmland, pastures, dry stone walls.
10. **Fern Gully** — tunnel of giant ferns and tree canopy, road winding down in green shade.
11. **Ocho Rios arrival** — turquoise sea bursts into view, resort strip, craft stalls, Dunn's River finale.
